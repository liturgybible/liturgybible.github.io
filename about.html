One-Tap Daily Readings

For the fastest access to the current day's first reading on your mobile device:

  1. Open liturgybible.org/today in your mobile browser.
  2. Tap the Share button (iOS) or Menu (Android).
  3. Select "Add to Home Screen".

This creates a shortcut on your home screen that instantly redirects to the day's first reading.

The annotations for the Liturgy Bible are compiled from a number of sources, including:

  • USCCB: Liturgical Calendar for the Dioceses of the United States of America
  • Felix Just, S.J., Ph.D.: The Catholic Lectionary Website (catholic-resources.org)
  • Liturgical Press: Liturgy and Life Study Bible, Edited by Paul Turner and John W. Martens
  • Matthew P Hazell: Index Lectionum: A Comparative Table of Readings for the Ordinary and Extraordinary Forms of the Roman Rite
  • 2020 Notitiae: M. Lessi Ariosto, Commento al ciclo biennale di letture bibliche nell’Officium lectionis della Liturgia Horarum, Notitiae 56 (2020) 132-179. [for the 2-year cycle of the office of readings, forthcoming]
  • Open Bibles: XML data for public domain biblical text, including KJV and DRA

The Liturgy Bible currently uses two public domain translations of the biblical text: the King James Version (KJV) and the Douay-Rheims Challoner (DRA). We have requested permission to use several liturgical translations, including the forthcoming updated NABRE (USA), the ESV-CE (UK), and the NRSV-CE (Canada).

This is a work in progress. If you see an error, you can submit a pull request or send an email.

Welcome to the Liturgy Bible, a tool for exploring today's Daily Readings for Catholic Mass in their full biblical context. Instead of viewing the readings as a list, this page links you directly to the Bible chapters where they are found.

As you read, notice the verses that come before and after the day's selection. Annotations in the margins show how the Church uses this part of Scripture throughout the year, both in the Lectionary for Mass (left margin) and the Liturgy of the Hours (right margin).

This site is a companion for study and prayer. For the official list of today's readings, we gratefully direct you to the USCCB Daily Readings page, which is the primary source for the text used at Mass in the United States. The Liturgy Bible is designed to be a complementary resource, perfect for diving deeper into the readings within the context of the whole Bible.
